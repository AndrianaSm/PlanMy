The start up project that our Venture Lab's group is working on is the creation of a local event planner application, called Plan My. Its main purpose is to simplify the planning of private events by showcasing some local services and venues, so that the client can find all the information about what they need in one place comprehensive of the budget and availability.\\
As the computer scientists of the group, we would like to work on the development of said application. We would start with a UX/UI study and design to then develop the project further with a more concrete Frontend and a simple Backend. 
Following the case study made together with the group, we are now working on a Figma wireframing that will be tested before passing to the next implementational part.\\
The prototype aims to be a minimum viable product of the application Plan My. It would include the basic features such as:
\begin{itemize}
    \item hosting different accounts
    \item allowing for the creation of events and their customisation (with the specification of name, type, date, location, estimated number of guests, and budget).
    \item having the possibility for the clients to search, filter and book some fictive services present in the database. The booking will be followed up by a status of the request and a messaging service between the client and the services providers.
    \item have information about the venues and the services with their own presenting pages
    \item review of the service once the event is concluded 
\end{itemize} 

The final outcome of the course would then be a software engineering project strengthen by the entrepreneurship hints learned through the workshop and the co-working with other people having a different background.
